Common questions

I reinstalled the app — is my progress gone?

No. As long as you're signed in to the same iCloud account, your scores, Delta balance, purchased themes and icons, and booster charges are restored automatically from iCloud. Make sure iCloud is enabled on your device.

I bought Delta but don't see it

Delta is added as soon as the App Store confirms the purchase. If it doesn't appear, check your internet connection and reopen the app — pending purchases are finished on launch. Delta packs are consumable, so they aren't "restored" like other purchases, but your balance is kept safe in iCloud.

How do the boosters work?

Each game starts with a set number of charges for Undo, Shuffle and Magic. Use them freely until they run out, then a new game refreshes them. You can buy extra starting charges with Delta in Settings.

How do I see the tutorial again?

Tap the info button (ⓘ) in the top bar, then "View tutorial again" at the bottom of the help screen.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your device — no connection needed to play. Your progress saves locally and syncs to iCloud the next time you're online.

Are there ads?

Never. 2048 Ultimate has no advertising and no tracking. Optional Delta packs are the only purchases.
