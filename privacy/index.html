Privacy Policy

2048 Ultimate · Last updated 8 November 2026

2048 Ultimate is a single‑player puzzle game built by an independent developer. It is designed to collect as little as possible — in fact, nothing about you is sent to us.

The short version: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no third‑party trackers in the app.

Information we collect

None, directly. 2048 Ultimate has no account system and no servers of its own. Your game data — scores, progress, Delta balance, purchased themes and icons, and settings — is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud account.

Apple services we rely on

Some features are provided by Apple. Any data they involve is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, not by us:

  • iCloud sync — your saved game is mirrored to your private iCloud (Key‑Value Storage) so it survives reinstalling the app and syncs across your devices. It stays in your Apple account; the developer has no access to it.
  • Game Center — if you use leaderboards and achievements, your scores and your Game Center nickname are managed by Apple and shown on Apple's rankings. You can disable Game Center in your device settings.
  • In‑app purchases — optional Delta packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment details; we never see or receive your payment information.

Analytics & advertising

There are none. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third‑party trackers. No usage data leaves your device to us.

Children's privacy

2048 Ultimate is suitable for all ages (rated 4+). It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove local and iCloud game data, delete the app and remove its data from iCloud in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
